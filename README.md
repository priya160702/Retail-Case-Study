# Retail Case Study

### Project Overview

The leadership team wanted a data-driven understanding of 2022 sales performance to identify:

1. Key customer segments

2. High-performing products

3. Top sales channels

The goal was to provide actionable insights for driving targeted growth in 2023.

This project leverages Excel-based data analysis to uncover temporal, demographic, geographic, and product trends and provide strategic recommendations.

### Objectives

Analyze temporal sales trends to identify seasonal opportunities

Segment customers based on demographics and purchasing behavior

Evaluate product performance across categories and colors

Identify top-performing sales channels

Provide strategic business recommendations for 2023 growth

### Approach & Solution
1. Temporal Trends

   Sales peaked in March, highlighting key seasonal demand

   Monthly sales trend visualized using line charts and conditional formatting

2. Customer Demographics

   Women contributed ~65% of total sales

   Age group 30–49 drove ~50% of revenue

   Analysis performed using pivot tables, grouping, and Excel formulas (SUMIFS, COUNTIFS)

3. Geographic Insights

   Top states: Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh (~35% of total sales)

   Used pivot tables with map charts for spatial visualization

4. Product Preferences

   Adult products in orange were most purchased

   Insights derived via product-category cross-tabulation and conditional formatting

5. Channel Analysis

   Amazon: ~35% of total sales

   Top three channels (Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra): ~80% of revenue

   Pivot tables & bar charts used to rank sales channels

### Key Insights

| Area | Key Insight |
|------|------------|
| Temporal Trends | Sales peak in March, indicating seasonal demand opportunities |
| Customer Demographics | Women aged 30–49 are the most valuable customer segment, contributing ~65% of sales |
| Geographic Trends | Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh account for ~35% of total sales |
| Product Preferences | Adult products in orange are the most purchased items |
| Channel Performance | Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra account for ~80% of total revenue, with Amazon alone contributing ~35% |

### Business Recommendations

1. Focus marketing campaigns on women aged 30–49

2. Target Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh with localized offers

3. Prioritize Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra for strategic promotions

4. Optimize inventory and marketing for high-demand periods, especially March

5. Integrate demographic, geographic, and channel insights for personalized campaigns

### Tools and Technologies

1. Microsoft Excel: Data cleaning, analysis, pivot tables, charts
2. Formulas: SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, VLOOKUP, IF statements
3. Visualization: Line charts, bar charts, map charts, conditional formatting
4. Analytical Methods: Segmentation, temporal trend analysis, channel performance evaluation

### Conclusion

This analysis provides a clear, actionable roadmap for revenue growth in 2023. It demonstrates the ability to:

Translate business questions into data-driven insights

Identify high-value customers, products, and channels

Make strategic recommendations to optimize marketing and sales performance
